The logic responds instantly, transitioning from the dynamic brake to the "coast" brake mode by forcing all six final PWM outputs to a low state. This successful, clean transition between two distinct braking strategies while the main brake remains engaged confirms the module's hierarchical control logic is functioning perfectly, providing a robust and flexible safety system for the motor controller.
